Gran Turismo 5 does the three-screen trick too, and it's the better version of the idea — no special cable, no hub, and it works in full GT mode rather than just an arcade sub-mode.

## What you need

- **Three PlayStation 3 consoles.**
- **Three copies of Gran Turismo 5**, all on the **same game version**. They do **not** need to share a PSN account.
- **A network switch or router.** I used an Ethernet switch and Cat5 cables; a router or Wi-Fi should also work, though I only tested it wired.
- Three screens.

![Three PlayStation 3 consoles wired up to three BenQ monitors, all booted to the PlayStation Store](/img/gt5-three-consoles-three-screens.jpg){: width="600"}

## Setup (all in-game)

1. On the middle console, go to **Settings > Hardware > Multi Monitor** and set it as the **server**. Note the IP address shown on that screen.
2. On the left console, go to the same **Settings > Hardware > Multi Monitor** menu, set it to **client**, enter the server's IP address, and set it as **Monitor 1**.
3. On the right console, do the same, setting it as **Monitor 3**.
4. Back on the server console, start a race. If it's set up correctly, the two side screens fade to black, then pick up their share of the view automatically.

The same menu also has Monitor Layout, Monitor Frame Border, Angle of Screen Curvature, and Adjust Angle of View settings — I left all of them at their defaults (3×1, 0%, 0°, and 100%) and didn't need to touch any of them to get a clean picture across the three screens.

![The Multi Monitor settings screen, set to Server with the console's IP address showing](/img/gt5-multi-monitor-settings-menu.jpg){: width="600"}

## The result

- It isn't limited to a single Arcade mode — I ran a Civic Sunday Cup race in full **GT mode** and the three-screen split kept working exactly the same as in Arcade, racing against CPU opponents.
- In fact, pretty much every mode in the game works across the three screens.
- Sync between the consoles is rock-solid.
- Apparently **five screens** is possible — I haven't tried it yet.
