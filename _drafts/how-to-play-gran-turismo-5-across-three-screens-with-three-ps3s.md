Gran Turismo 5 does the three-screen trick too, and it's the better version of the idea — no special cable, no hub, and it works in full career mode rather than just an arcade sub-mode.

## What you need

- **Three PlayStation 3 consoles.**
- **Three copies of Gran Turismo 5**, all on the **same game version**. They do **not** need to share a PSN account.
- **A network switch or router.** Wired is ideal; Wi-Fi should work.
- Three screens.

## Setup (all in-game)

1. Pick one console as the **centre screen / server**. Note its **IP address**.
2. On the other two consoles, set the display role to **Monitor 1** (left) and **Monitor 3** (right), and point each at the server's IP address.
3. Start a race from the server console. The two side screens fade to black, then pick up their share of the view automatically.

## Why the PS3 version is better

- It isn't limited to a single Arcade mode. **Full simulation / career mode, licence tests, and even races against CPU opponents** all work correctly split across the three screens.
- Sync between the consoles is rock-solid.
- Apparently **five screens** is possible — I haven't tried it yet.
