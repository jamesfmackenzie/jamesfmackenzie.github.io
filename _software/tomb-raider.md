Tomb Raider is one of the DOS games with a genuine [Glide]({% link _software/glide.md %}) patch, which makes it a proper native-DOS Voodoo test rather than something running through Windows 98. There are two patches — a statically-linked one that only works with Voodoo 1 cards, and a dynamically-linked one (sometimes called the Voodoo Rush patch) that works across the whole Voodoo range. The dynamic one defaults a little dark; gamma can be adjusted via environment variables, though that fix only reliably works on Voodoo 1/2, not Voodoo 3 and later.

Running it on real hardware is genuinely worth it: on a [Voodoo 2]({% post_url 2026-08-30-voodoo-2-on-a-modern-pc %}), Tomb Raider's underwater sections look noticeably better accelerated than on the PS1 or the DOS software renderer.
