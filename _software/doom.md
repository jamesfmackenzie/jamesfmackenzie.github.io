Doom is the standard first test on every DOS machine I put together — see the [OptiPlex 380 retro build]({% post_url 2025-12-28-the-ugly-dell-optiplex-that-became-my-dream-retro-gaming-pc %}), where it was, unsurprisingly, the first thing tried and it worked beautifully. It's the reliable benchmark too: software rendering performance in Doom is one of the numbers I actually track across different retro PCs.

It's also a good general-MIDI test case alongside [mt32-pi]({% link _software/mt32-pi.md %}) and the [Dreamblaster X2/X2GS]({% link _hardware/dreamblaster-x2gs.md %}) — the soundtrack holds up well across FM synth, MT-32, and General MIDI playback.

And it's been ported and modded in every direction imaginable, from software-rendered voxel remakes ([Voxel Doom]({% link _software/voxel-doom.md %})) to [Commander Keen]({% link _software/commander-keen.md %})-style WebAssembly browser ports of other id Software-era classics.
