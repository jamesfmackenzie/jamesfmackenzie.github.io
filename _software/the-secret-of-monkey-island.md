LucasArts' The Secret of Monkey Island is the game I keep reaching for to demonstrate DOS-era audio hardware, because its soundtrack was built with exactly that range of hardware in mind — I've used it to show off [PC speaker vs. AdLib vs. Roland MT-32 audio]({% post_url 2021-03-20-the-secret-of-monkey-island-soundtrack-pc-speaker-vs-adlib-vs-roland-mt32-mt-32-audio %}) side by side, and again for [Roland MT-32 playback via MUNT]({% post_url 2022-04-09-the-secret-of-monkey-island-soundtrack-roland-mt32-mt-32 %}) on the [ao486]({% link _software/ao486.md %}) core through MiSTer's MidiLink.
