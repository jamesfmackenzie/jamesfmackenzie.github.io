Voxel Doom replaces [Doom]({% link _software/doom.md %})'s classic 2D sprites with true voxel models, rendered in real time rather than the billboarded flat images the original engine used. It's a strange thing to see on paper — an authentic upgrade, or messing with something that didn't need it — but [in motion it genuinely works]({% post_url 2022-09-02-doom-made-with-voxels %}), giving enemies and pickups real depth without losing the low-poly, period-correct look.
