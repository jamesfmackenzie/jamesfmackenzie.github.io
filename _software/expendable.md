Expendable is a Glide and OpenGL-capable third-person shooter from the Voodoo era. It's one of the two titles I used — alongside Quake III — to confirm that the [3dfx Voodoo Velocity 100]({% link _hardware/3dfx-voodoo-velocity-100.md %})'s second-TMU unlock actually worked. With the fix applied, the card performs virtually identically to a Voodoo 3 2000 in both games — effectively becoming the card it was cut down from.
