Final Reality is the benchmark Remedy Entertainment released in May 1997, in cooperation with VNU Labs — and it's the direct ancestor of [3DMark]({% link _software/3dmark.md %}), which took over the same role for every generation of 3D hardware after it.

I have genuinely vivid memories of running it back then, on a [3D Rage Pro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATI_Rage_series#3D_RAGE_Pro), an [Intel i740](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intel740), and a [3dfx Voodoo 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voodoo2) — the exact generation of cards it was built to push.
