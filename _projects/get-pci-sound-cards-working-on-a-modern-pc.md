I want to play original DOS games stupidly fast, with sound from a real late-1990s sound card. ISA cards are out, and a period PCI sound card in a modern PC just gets you silence. This project is about finding the fastest PC that can still run a real 1990s sound card in native DOS, and getting the cards working in it with real hardware, not emulation.

### Project Notes

Status | In progress
Goal | Find the fastest PC that can run a late-1990s PCI sound card in native DOS, and get DOS games playing through it in hardware, not emulation.
